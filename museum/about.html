關於 MuseumAbout Museum

StarRiver Museum 是一項持續發展中的數位策展與創作計畫。

StarRiver Museum is an evolving digital curation and creative project.

我們希望用展覽、文字、互動體驗與虛擬空間，組織不同形式的作品、觀察與故事。研究只是其中一種方法；作品與文化內容不需要全部以研究的形式發表。

Through exhibitions, writing, interactive experiences, and virtual spaces, Museum brings together works, observations, and stories. Research is one approach among many, not a requirement for all content.

網站可參觀；虛擬館舍持續規劃Public web displays; venue in development

目前已有可進入的網頁展示。獨立 VR 館舍仍在規劃，並未以完整開館的狀態對外宣告。

Browser-accessible displays are available now. A separate VR venue remains in development and is not presented as fully open.

網站與館舍Site and venue

入口不等於建築The site is not the building

Museum 網站是策展與內容入口；虛擬館舍是其中一種空間體驗。展覽可以在網頁、獨立小站、VRChat 世界或其他媒介發生，不必先進入同一棟建築。

The Museum website is a curatorial entry point; the virtual venue is one possible spatial experience. Exhibitions may live on the web, in standalone sites, in VRChat worlds, or in other media.

呈現方式What belongs here

作品、內容與空間Works, writing, and spaces

除了作品展示，這裡也可以容納文化文章、社群記錄、創作筆記、研究與互動體驗。不同內容會依實際發表情形逐步建立入口，不預先假設固定展期或作品數量。

Alongside artworks, Museum can host cultural writing, community accounts, creative notes, research, and interactive experiences. Pages will follow actual published content rather than a pre-set schedule or quota.
