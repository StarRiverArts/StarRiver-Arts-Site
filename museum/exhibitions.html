Museum gathers entry points to available displays and works. An exhibition can have its own pages, visual language, address, or VR space; this directory helps visitors find it.

內容導覽Viewing guide

這組資料可以看什麼？What can you explore?

這裡呈現的是虛擬展場設計方案、圖文參考與互動測試工具，不是已建成館舍的導覽，也不代表所有規格均經實地驗證。

These are virtual gallery design proposals, references, and interactive study tools—not a tour of a completed building or a claim that every specification has been field-validated.

01 / Design reference

認識空間設計的構成Read the spatial system

包含色彩、材質、人因、展品留白與預留量體、曲線、圖面、照明、虛擬效果，以及字體與標識設計。

Explore color, material, ergonomics, spacing, reserve volumes, curves, plans, lighting, VR effects, typography, and signage.

02 / Interactive study

以工具觀察觀看條件Explore conditions interactively

分別從展牆與觀賞距離的配置，以及照明衰減與表面顏色的變化，理解虛擬展場設計所面對的問題。

Use layout and light-falloff tools to explore viewing distance, clearance, illumination, and surface color.
