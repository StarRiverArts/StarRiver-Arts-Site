Season format

賽季與日常活動Seasons and regular meetsシーズンと活動

VRRCTW 以半年為一個賽季，每季通常安排五至六輪，每輪包含 Sacc 與 CVS 兩場比賽，合計約十至十二場。活動原則上每月舉辦；實際日期、場地、車輛指定或限制與規則，以當期公告為準。

VRRCTW generally runs a half-year season with five to six rounds. Each round includes one Sacc and one CVS race, for roughly ten to twelve races per season. Dates, locations, vehicle restrictions and rules follow the current announcement.

Submission

提交 Time Attack 紀錄Submit a Time Attack record

Time Attack 目前只能透過 Discord 投稿。最低資料為一張清楚的 VRChat 截圖，畫面需要能辨識完成時間、使用車種、FPS，以及計時面板上的其他必要資訊。

Time Attack submissions currently go through Discord only. At minimum, provide a clear VRChat screenshot showing the finish time, vehicle, FPS and other required timing-panel information.

所有投稿都會經過人工確認後才加入一般榜；正式榜則依不同世界與路線採用各自的正式收錄規則。

Every submission is manually reviewed before entering the general records. Official-board requirements vary by world and route.

Creator

StarRiver Arts

StarRiver Arts 是專注於臺灣地景的場景創作者，作品包含 ProjectT 臺灣公路地景系列，以及多個太空與展覽相關主題世界。創作的目標，是讓大家在 VRChat 中有更多地方可以探索、駕駛、聚會與遊玩。

StarRiver Arts creates environments focused on Taiwanese landscapes, including the ProjectT road-world series and several space- and exhibition-themed worlds, with the goal of giving people more places to explore, drive, meet and play in VRChat.
