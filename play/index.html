世界、社群、資料與工具Worlds, community, data, and tools

ProjectT 包含 VRChat 世界、中文賽車社群、計時與賽事資料，以及活動工具。玩家可以在這裡找到世界、參與活動、查詢紀錄，並了解社群、賽道與賽事的發展。

ProjectT brings together VRChat worlds, a Chinese-language racing community, timing and event data, and competition tools. Find worlds, join activities, explore records, and follow the development of the community and its racing culture.

VRChat Racing Toolkit

協助活動組織者設定賽事、整理結果並串接 Racing 的工具組。計時榜資料串接等部分核心流程已投入內部使用，完整的公開操作介面、發布板與資料整合仍在開發中。

A competition toolset for event setup, result processing, and Racing integration. Core timing-data workflows are in internal use while the public interface remains in development.
