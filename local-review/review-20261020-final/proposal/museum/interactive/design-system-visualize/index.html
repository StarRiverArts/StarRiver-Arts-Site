移動一面牆，或把燈光拉遠，觀看的感受會如何改變？親手試試空間與光線的不同組合。

Move a wall or pull a light farther away. How does the experience change? Try different combinations of space and light.

光與距離Light & Distance

把光源拉近或推遠，再換一種表面顏色，觀察亮度與色彩如何變化。

Move the light closer or farther away, then change the surface colour to see how brightness and colour shift.
