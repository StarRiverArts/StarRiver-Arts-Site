StarRiver Arts / Museum

StarRiver Museum

一個讓作品、故事與不同媒介相遇的數位策展空間。

A developing curatorial space where works, stories, and media meet.

從網頁上的互動展示，到文化內容與未來的虛擬館舍，Museum 將不同形式的創作與觀看經驗組織在一起。每檔展覽也可以保留自己的網站、空間與呈現方式。

Museum brings together web-based displays, cultural writing, and developing virtual spaces. Each exhibition can retain its own website, spatial setting, and form of presentation.
